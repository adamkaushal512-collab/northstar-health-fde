# Phase 23 — Expansion / Additional Use Cases

The initial vertical slice remains MRI/CT prior-authorization evidence investigation.

Expansion is modeled through an explicit use-case registry. A candidate second use case is specialty-medication prior authorization evidence investigation. It remains disabled until its workflow, payer requirements, evidence model, evaluation dataset, red-team cases, guardrails, and UAT criteria are independently validated.

Reusable platform components include adapters, provenance contracts, retrieval/evaluation infrastructure, guardrails, observability, deployment controls, and human handoff patterns. Business rules and clinical evidence requirements remain use-case specific.

Expansion therefore reuses platform capabilities without assuming one workflow's safety evidence automatically transfers to another.
