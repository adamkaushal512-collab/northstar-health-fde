# Phase 22 — Business Impact Measurement

Technical success is not sufficient; the deployment must demonstrate operational value.

NorthStar defines before/after measures for investigation resolution time, systems searched per case, escalation rate, repeat-contact rate, throughput, and quality/safety guardrails.

The included baseline is synthetic demonstration data and must not be represented as a real customer outcome. In a production pilot, measurement would use an agreed baseline window, comparable cohorts, sufficient sample size, and explicit exclusion/quality rules.

Impact reporting must pair efficiency metrics with safety and quality metrics so faster processing cannot hide worse decisions.
